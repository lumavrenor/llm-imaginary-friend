# Pinned pronunciations for the English edition ([kokoro] lexicon_file in config.en.toml).
#
#   One "word<TAB>IPA" per line. Lines starting with # and blank lines are ignored.
#   Case doesn't matter.
#
# Why this exists
#   Words missing from the dictionary get an estimated pronunciation. It never breaks, but it
#   isn't always right (Nene comes out as "nay-nay", Io as "eye-oh"). The girls' names and words
#   that come up all the time are pinned here.
#
# How to write IPA (Kokoro's symbol set)
#   Stress marks go right before the vowel: ˈ (primary) ˌ (secondary).
#   Diphthongs are single letters: A=eɪ  I=aɪ  O=oʊ  W=aʊ  Y=ɔɪ
#   Common consonants: ʧ=ch  ʤ=j  ʃ=sh  ʒ=zh  θ=th (thin)  ð=th (this)  ŋ=ng  ɹ=r
#   Unknown words are logged as WARNING with their estimated reading while the app runs;
#   fixing that estimate and pasting it here is the quickest way.
